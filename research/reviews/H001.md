**KILL as a clean validation of the stated hypothesis.** I found no signal lookahead, but the card and tested exit rule differ, the historical “holdout” was previously exposed, and funding accounting remains approximate. Poor profitability alone is not my reason.

1. **Lookahead: no violation found.** The signal uses the current close-to-close log return, the previous 720 returns for sigma, and the previous 168 volumes for the median. Current-bar volume is available at its close. Funding is joined backward against `close_time`, excluding later settlements ([hypotheses.py:16–35](../../opusjev/hypotheses.py#L16)). The engine processes existing orders before generating signals; pending entries fill at the following bar’s open ([engine.py:48–87](../../opusjev/engine.py#L48), [broker.py:122–132](../../opusjev/broker.py#L122)). ATR includes the signal bar, which is permissible.

2. **Splits do not overlap, but historical independence is compromised.** Current boundaries are dev `[2022-10-16, 2025-07-01)` and OOS `[2025-07-01, 2026-10-16)`. Data are truncated before feature calculation, with legitimate earlier history retained for indicators ([lab.py:52](../../opusjev/lab.py#L52), [lab.py:101–126](../../opusjev/lab.py#L101)).

   However, earlier research selected parameters using the last 365 days and evaluated the preceding years; completed results are documented ([alt_signals.py:156–173](../../research/alt_signals.py#L156), the internal project notes). This exposed the card’s July–October 2026 “holdout.” A model knowledge cutoff cannot undo that exposure. **Current v2 correctly reserves a new, prospective holdout starting October 16**; I found no evidence that this future period leaked. Its historical OOS nevertheless remains adaptively reused research data.

3. **Endpoint closure fixes trade censoring, but leaves stale equity metrics.** V2 closes remaining positions after `run_backtest`, then constructs equity from the previously recorded curve ([lab.py:130–144](../../opusjev/lab.py#L130)). Forced-closing slippage and fees enter trade PnL but never update that curve. Whenever positions remain at the endpoint, PF/expectancy include costs omitted from return, drawdown and Sharpe. I could not establish whether this affected this particular H001 run.

4. **Funding sign is correct; accounting is incomplete.** Positive funding is deducted from longs and credited to shorts. But every settlement uses `qty × entry_price`, and funding reaches reported equity only at trade exit ([lab.py:134–144](../../opusjev/lab.py#L134)). Broker equity, subsequent sizing and loss-dependent controls therefore exclude funding during the simulation ([risk.py:71–82](../../opusjev/risk.py#L71), [risk.py:115–157](../../opusjev/risk.py#L115)). These are retrospectively funding-adjusted results, not a settlement-aware portfolio simulation.

5. **Execution costs and selection broadly match their descriptions.** The model charges fees and adverse slippage, prioritizes stops when both exits are touched, and requires target penetration ([config.py:35–39](../../opusjev/config.py#L35), [broker.py:138–186](../../opusjev/broker.py#L138)). Fixed slippage provides no validation of execution during flushes. `pf_ex_fees` appropriately still includes slippage and funding.

   Selection takes maximum dev net PF among variants with at least 40 trades, matching the supplied choice ([lab.py:232–240](../../opusjev/lab.py#L232)). Uncertainty is weaker: bootstrap resamples individual trades independently, ignoring correlated events; DSR counts earlier trials but estimates variance only from recorded dev Sharpe values ([metrics.py:21–26](../../opusjev/metrics.py#L21), [lab.py:189–192](../../opusjev/lab.py#L189)).

6. **Concentration remains a material limitation.** From the supplied rows, also present at [ledger.jsonl:25–27](../../research/ledger.jsonl#L25):

   - Selected dev is 78.1% long; OOS is 68.9% long. Both OOS sides lose.
   - OOS has 161 trades across ten symbols, so it is not literally a tiny total sample.
   - BTC and ETH contribute **−$678.39**; the other eight symbols together contribute **+$106.10**. Aggregate losses are therefore strongly concentrated in two symbols.
   - Individual-trade and regime concentration remain unresolved: reported aggregates omit trade tails, event clusters and subperiod breakdowns. Daily kurtosis of 31.14 flags heavy tails but does not identify their source.

7. **The code tests a liquidation proxy under a different exit specification.** Price shock, volume and funding-sign conditions match the card, but nothing measures liquidations, forced-selling share or counterparty identity. Any result would concern that proxy, not establish the claimed causal mechanism.

   More concretely, the card explicitly says the time stop is conditional on never reaching half-target ([H001.toml:17–18](../../research/cards/H001.toml#L17)). V2 instead sets `hard_time_stop=True`, forcing exit after `hold` bars ([lab.py:129](../../opusjev/lab.py#L129)). Funding blackouts, same-side caps and loss-dependent sizing also remain active. The reported experiment therefore differs from the card’s full specification.
