**I found reasons to reject this as independent validation of H003, although I found no signal lookahead.** The negative profitability alone is not my reason for KILL.

1. **Signal timing appears causal.** `h003_btc_lead` uses current close-to-close log returns, divided by a 720-bar standard deviation shifted one bar backward. BTC is aligned to the alt’s same `open_time`; volume and funding are unused, and BTC receives zero signals ([hypotheses.py:22–23,59–74](../../opusjev/hypotheses.py#L59)). The engine processes the current bar before submitting orders; pending orders fill at the following bar’s open ([engine.py:48–87](../../opusjev/engine.py#L48), [broker.py:122–150](../../opusjev/broker.py#L122)). I checked the ten CSVs: their timestamps match, with no missing or duplicate hours or malformed bar durations.

2. **The “OOS” period was already exposed.** The nominal split is dev before 2025-07-01 and OOS thereafter. The module explicitly acknowledges that **all historical months had already been examined in earlier research** ([lab.py:10–11,52–58](../../opusjev/lab.py#L10)). Earlier research reports also describe examining 2025–26 results and comparing alternatives (the internal project notes). Thus, this is reused evaluation data, not an untouched test. Adding previous trials to deflated Sharpe does not restore independence from hypothesis-generation decisions.

   The mechanical boundaries themselves look sound: bars require `close_time < end`, trading requires `open_time >= start`, and each split starts a fresh engine. Earlier history supplies causal feature warmup ([lab.py:101–130](../../opusjev/lab.py#L101)). The future holdout begins 2026-10-16; I found no evidence that its observations entered this result.

3. **The harness tests an undocumented, order-dependent subset of the card’s trades.** It disables three loss thresholds but retains consecutive-loss cooldowns and half sizing, daily entry limits, funding-hour blackouts, and a maximum of two positions in the same direction ([lab.py:119–120](../../opusjev/lab.py#L119), [risk.py:71–82,99–136](../../opusjev/risk.py#L71)). Candidates are considered in card-symbol order, and accepted pending orders immediately consume those limits ([engine.py:57–87](../../opusjev/engine.py#L57)). Since H003 often signals multiple alts in BTC’s direction, earlier-listed symbols receive systematic priority. These statistics therefore describe the signal plus portfolio filters, rather than the card’s stated rule alone.

4. **Funding sign and basic execution costs are correct, but accounting is approximate.** Positive funding is subtracted from longs and credited to shorts. However, funding uses entry notional throughout and is applied only after the simulation, so it never affects subsequent sizing or loss-based decisions ([lab.py:134–144](../../opusjev/lab.py#L134)). Fees and adverse slippage are charged; stop checks precede TP checks, and TP requires trade-through ([config.py:35–39](../../opusjev/config.py#L35), [broker.py:160–186](../../opusjev/broker.py#L160)).

   A latent endpoint bug remains: period-end liquidation occurs after the last equity observation, without updating that observation, potentially omitting liquidation costs from return, drawdown and Sharpe ([lab.py:130–145](../../opusjev/lab.py#L130)). For this H003 row, symbol PnL totals approximately −$118.97 and matches reported return, so I found no material endpoint discrepancy here.

5. **Selection matches the stated rule; statistical robustness does not follow.** Among variants with at least 40 dev trades, the code selects maximum dev net PF. That correctly selects `k=3.5, hold=3`, despite every variant losing ([lab.py:232–240](../../opusjev/lab.py#L232)). The bootstrap independently resamples individual trades, ignoring dependence between alts exposed to the same BTC shock ([metrics.py:21–26](../../opusjev/metrics.py#L21)). Its confidence interval therefore does not establish robustness across independent events or regimes.

6. **The limited favorable evidence is concentrated.** The OOS row contains 100 trades across eight symbols: 56 longs with PF 0.657 and 44 shorts with PF 1.175. Only SOL (+$96.71) and LTC (+$13.01) have positive symbol-level net PnL; SOL supplies **88.1% of that positive symbol-level contribution**. Removing SOL’s contribution leaves approximately −$215.68 ([ledger.jsonl:39](../../research/ledger.jsonl#L39)). The ledger lacks individual-trade and regime breakdowns, so it cannot rule out a few trades or clustered events explaining those favorable pockets. Overall PF 0.855 and expectancy lower bound −0.119R already fail the card’s thresholds.

7. **The formula matches; the causal mechanism remains untested.** The code implements the card’s signed inequalities, 3×ATR stop, 3R price target and hard holding limit. But simultaneous hourly returns cannot establish that BTC moved first, identify stale quotes, or attribute subsequent returns to liquidity-provider catch-up. The long condition also admits strongly falling alts during BTC rallies, as the literal formula permits ([hypotheses.py:69–74](../../opusjev/hypotheses.py#L69)). This tests a return-divergence proxy, not the claimed causal sequence.
